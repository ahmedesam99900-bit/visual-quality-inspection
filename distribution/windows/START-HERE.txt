Visual Inspection Studio for Windows x64

Open Visual Inspection Studio from the Windows Start menu. Select Load sample
image and Run inspection to try the included PCB1 model. For this model, use
consistently framed crops of the HC-SR04 board.

Use Products & datasets to add a product and upload labeled images. Train a
new version in Training & models, review its metrics, and activate it before
running inspection.

For cloud training, choose Help > Connect Modal GPU and sign in to your own
Modal workspace. Reopen the studio after connecting.

Workspaces are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\VisualInspectionStudio\workspace.
The diagnostic log is available through Help > Open diagnostic log.

The built-in sample and benchmark data are from VisA PCB1 by Yang Zou,
Jongheon Jeong, Latha Pemula, Dongqing Zhang and Onkar Dabeer, CC BY 4.0.
Dataset: https://github.com/amazon-science/spot-diff
